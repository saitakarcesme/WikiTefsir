# WikiTefsir Gallery — 100 source-based scene briefs

These are production briefs for a later image-generation pass. Every scene must be treated as an artistic environment rather than a portrait.

## Shared visual direction

- Museum-quality cinematic painting, restrained color, natural light, historically plausible materials, rich atmospheric depth, no text inside the image.
- Never show the face of a prophet, messenger, Companion, angel, or revered person. Use distant scale, rear view, silhouette, veiling light, architecture, hands, footprints, clothing, or an empty environment.
- Never depict Allah. Do not turn unseen matters into literal portraits. Angels may be represented only through light, wind, shadow, or an implied presence with no bodily face.
- Avoid modern objects, fantasy armor, orientalist stereotypes, halos, icons, and invented religious symbols.
- The source line controls the scene. Details not stated by the source should remain visually neutral.
